# Falling Words

A small typing game I made using HTML CSS JavaScript.

Words fall from the top of the screen..

and you have to type them before they reach the bottom...

The longer you survive... the harder it gets.

## How to Play

1. A word will appear and start falling.
2. Type the word using keyboard.
3. Finish typing it to destroy it and earn points....
4. If a word reaches the bottom.. you lose a life. ( ishowwwspeeeed) ahhahaha
5. You have 3 lives. Once they're gone, You looseee

Try to beat your previous high score !!!!!!!!!

## Controls

 1. use the 26 alphabet letters for falling words
 2. Backspace for erasing the letter ( not word)
 3. ESC to skip the word u typin
 4. SHIFT + P to pause or resume

You can also use the Pause button on the screen....

## Features

- Typing several words in a row without missing one increases your combo and earns you more points.
- The words start falling faster as your combo increases.... New words also appear more often.
- Missing a word costs one life......
- Shows how fast you're typing.
- Shows how many of your letter attempts were correct.
- Small sounds for typing... completing words... and losing lives....
   You can turn them on or off.
- Your highest score is saved in your browser...so you can try to beat it next time.


I also added a small burning effect when a word is completed.

## How Scoring Works

The first word gives you 10 points.

Every word you complete in the same combo gives you 2 more points than the previous one.

If you miss a word, your combo goes back to zero.
